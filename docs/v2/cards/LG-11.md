# LG-11: the redaction driver with spans, same behaviour

**Plan:** section 6.1 (design 7), section 8 Stage 1 row LG-11. **Design:** `docs/v2/working-papers/design/ledger.md` section 10 (10.1 to 10.4) and its row LG-11 in section 20. **Size:** M. **Reviewers:** two (redaction is privacy-bearing).

## Why

`redact.Redact` returns a string and nothing else: no caller can tell what was removed or where, a long text cannot be stopped at a deadline with a safe prefix, and no rule can be measured. Every later change to redaction (the corpus and rates of LG-12, the policies of LG-26) needs a driver that returns spans. This package builds that driver and changes no behaviour at all.

## What to build

In `internal/redact`, beside what is there:

1. **`Scan`**: one pass over a text that returns the spans the rules would replace, each with its start, its end, the rule or kind that found it and a tier label, in order and without overlap. It takes a context and stops at its deadline, returning the spans found so far and how far it read, so that the caller can keep a redacted prefix and drop the rest.
2. **`Apply`**: turns a text and its spans into the redacted text.
3. **`Redact` becomes a wrapper** over the two and returns exactly what it returns today, for every input: the union of what the frozen baseline rules and the current rules remove.
4. **No rule changes.** Not one pattern, threshold or marker moves. `internal/redact/baseline` is not edited, moved or re-exported. Tiers are labels only and change nothing.

## Owns and may touch

Non-baseline files of `internal/redact`, and its tests.

## Must not touch

`internal/redact/baseline/**`, in any way. Every caller of `redact.Redact` outside the package. The docs.

## Accepted when

- `scripts/linux-check.sh` passes, with every existing redaction test unchanged and passing.
- A differential test holds `Apply(Scan(x)) == Redact_v1(x)` on every case of the existing tests and on at least 150,000 generated inputs, where `Redact_v1` is the old function kept under another name in a test file. The generator mixes secret shapes from the existing tests with ordinary text, code, URLs, `key: value` lines and long lines, from a fixed seed, and the test runs inside the project's time limits without depending on the machine's speed.
- Idempotence holds: redacting a redacted text changes nothing, on the same inputs.
- A cancelled scan returns a prefix that is safe: by test, for cancellation at generated points, the kept prefix after `Apply` contains no secret the full `Redact` would have removed from that prefix, and nothing after the point is kept. The slow side of that test blocks until the test releases it; no sleep decides the outcome.
- The benchmark shows capture is not slower. If a faster driver falls out of the single pass, report the numbers from alternating runs; do not chase speed at the cost of the differential test.
- `git diff --stat main -- internal/redact/baseline` is empty.
